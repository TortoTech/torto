## Improvement

- Sync the book you are reading first, then other books in recent-reading order, with quieter automatic synchronization on desktop and Android.
- Simplify Android reading controls, typography and theme panels, settings and statistics, with clearer icons and smoother transitions.
- Redesign Android reading assistant and feedback dialogs for full-screen input, with clearer book citations and conversations retained during the current reading session.
- Improve text selection and annotation input, merge overlapping highlights, and offer removal when selecting an existing highlight on desktop and Android.
- Improve ebook compatibility for contents, metadata, covers, embedded fonts and images across EPUB, MOBI, FB2 and comic archives.
- Keep original heading recognition while preventing AI layout from turning ordinary paragraphs into headings.

- Keep captions, sources and explanatory notes with their figures and tables, and remove unnecessary spacing around them.

- Restore the previous focused passage when returning to a section, and prevent gestures from being lost while the next section loads.
