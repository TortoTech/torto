# English hyphenation dictionaries

`en-us.standard.bincode` and `en-gb.standard.bincode` are the compiled
standard dictionaries generated by `hyphenation` 0.8.4 from the hyph-utf8
English patterns. They are bundled separately so Torto only ships the two
locales it supports.

The American English patterns are copyright Gerard D. C. Kuiken. Copying and
distribution, with or without modification, are permitted in any medium
without royalty provided the copyright and permission notices are preserved.

The British English patterns originate from the hyph-utf8/TeX hyphenation
collection and retain their original permissive distribution terms.
